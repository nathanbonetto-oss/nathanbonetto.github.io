Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Nathan eBay Sales Tracker is a personal application used to retrieve and organize eBay order information for the account owner's private sales tracking.

Information We Access

With your authorization, the application accesses eBay order and fulfillment information through the eBay API. This may include order numbers, transaction details, item descriptions, prices, shipping information, and order dates.

How Information Is Used

This information is used solely to organize and display sales data in a private tracking spreadsheet. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored securely to allow authorized API access. Access is restricted to the application owner.

Data Retention and Deletion

Stored information may be deleted by the application owner at any time. Users may revoke application access through their eBay account settings.
